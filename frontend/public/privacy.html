Maestro — Privacy Note

Effective 2026-07-23 · Private alpha

What we store

  • Account: your handle, your password in hashed form (never the password itself), and your credit balance and history.
  • What you create: your requests, the game specifications drafted from them, and the generated games and their assets.
  • Operations: logs needed to run, debug, and protect the Service — build activity, resource usage, content-safety screening, and standard server logs.

What we don't do

  • No analytics, tracking pixels, or advertising of any kind.
  • No selling or sharing of your data, except with the infrastructure that runs the Service and where the law requires it.
  • No third-party AI services: generation runs on models we operate ourselves. Your prompts are not sent to outside AI providers.
  • No payment data — the alpha has no payments.

Cookies

One essential cookie signs your browser into the game player. No tracking cookies.

Retention

Data is kept while your account exists. During the private alpha, account and data removal is handled directly; a formal contact path will be provided in the future.

See also the terms of service.
