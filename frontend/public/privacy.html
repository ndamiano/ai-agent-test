Maestro — Privacy Note

Effective 2026-07-23 · Private alpha

What we store

  • Account: your handle and a salted hash of your password (never the password itself), session tokens (stored hashed), and your credit ledger.
  • What you create: your chat messages and game requests, the game specifications drafted from them, the generated games and their assets, and build logs/events.
  • Usage: per-build GPU time (that's how compute budgets are metered) and standard server logs (including IP addresses) kept by our hosting.
  • Safety: if a request trips the illegal-content filter we log which term matched and your account id — never the full text of the request.

What we don't do

  • No analytics, tracking pixels, or advertising of any kind.
  • No selling or sharing of your data, except the infrastructure that runs the Service (our host, and cloud GPU workers that process generation jobs) and where the law requires it.
  • No third-party AI services: generation runs on models we operate ourselves. Your prompts are not sent to outside AI providers.
  • No payment data — the alpha has no payments. (When payments arrive they'll be handled by a payment provider; card details will never touch our servers.)

Cookies

One essential cookie (maestro_play) signs your browser into the game player. No tracking cookies. Your session token is kept in your browser's storage.

Retention and deletion

Data is kept while your account exists. Email us to delete your account and its data — generated games included. Backups may lag deletion by a short period.
