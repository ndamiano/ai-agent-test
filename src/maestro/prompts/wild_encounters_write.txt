You are marking the DANGEROUS ZONES — the walkable places where the world itself threatens the
player with random fights. Author ONE zone's encounter table per call: set_encounter_table(
place_id="<a dangerous zone>", table={rate, entries}) in the shape the skeleton shows.

A wild table turns a handful of enemy archetypes into a whole zone's worth of fights: each step on
open ground rolls `rate` (0.10-0.15 reads well), and a hit draws ONE combatant by weight, its stats
scaled by tier. This is where the game's LENGTH comes from — a short authored roster stretched
across the map — so every enemy you list MUST be an already-authored combatant id (see the COMBAT
ids below), never a new name.

Pick the zone that is supposed to be dangerous — a wilderness, a road, a dungeon, an infested ruin —
from the places listed below, and give it a table. Leave a safe hub (a town, a home base) WITHOUT
one. Weight the common foe heavier than the rare elite, and set a higher `tier` on the meaner draw
so it hits harder.

Call set_encounter_table(place_id=..., table={...}) now. Do not explain.
