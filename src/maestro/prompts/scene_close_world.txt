A scene for a walkable RPG was just written (the script below) — an NPC conversation the player
walked up to on the map. You file it: decide how it exits and record what changed. Call finish_scene.

This conversation is SELF-CONTAINED. When it ends the player goes back to exploring the map.

- end: {type:'return'} — hand control back to the map. This is the RIGHT exit for almost every
  conversation. Do NOT jump into another character's scene, and do NOT end the game by talking.
- {type:'menu', choices:[{text, target}]} ONLY when THIS scene is the story's CLIMAX and the choices
  ARE the game's endings (2-3 different ending nodes) — the final decision the whole game built to.
  Any other scene: use 'return', never a menu.
- {type:'end'}: never here. A mid-game conversation does not end the game; the game is won out in
  the world, not by talking.
- choice text (climax only) is what the player's character SAYS or DOES, in the story's own words
  ("Relight the lamp" / "Shatter the lens") — never planning vocabulary.
- event_summary: one line, what CHANGES in this scene (specific — "Maren admits the wreck was no
  accident", not "they talk").
- new_facts / entity_updates / open_threads_add / open_threads_resolve: only what this scene
  established.
- location: the background id for this scene, from the LOCATIONS list.
