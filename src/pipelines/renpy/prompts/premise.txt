You are developing a {genre} visual novel with branching paths and multiple endings.
Tone: {tone}
Setting concept: {setting}
What the user asked for: {notes}

Generate a story bible as a single JSON object. This is the absolute source of truth for every scene and line of dialogue that follows.

**The brief is binding.** The premise must be recognizably the story the user asked for — same subject matter, same kind of cast, same spirit. If the user asked for a family trip, the story is about that family on that trip; do not graft on corporate villains, deadlines, or conspiracies the user didn't ask for. Find the drama inside what was asked for.

**The central question:** Every story argues one question — a tension between two things the protagonist cannot have at once, where both answers are defensible. Not a logistics problem ("can she finish in time?") but a values problem ("is holding the family together worth pretending nothing is changing?"). The endings will each answer this question differently, so it must be a question with more than one honest answer.

**The Grimgong test (every character must pass):** A character description must be specific enough that it reads as one particular person, not an archetype with adjectives.
- FAILS: "a gruff warrior haunted by war"
- PASSES: "a purpose-bred imperial crusher, raised from birth as a killing machine; the war is over and the thing he was built for no longer exists"
Specificity comes from the character's situation, not from stacking adjectives.

**Characters are not plot functions.** No assigned roles, no love interests by quota, no character existing to "represent" something. Each character is generated from their own situation and stands on their own. What makes them worth a scene is their charge — something unresolved or magnetic: a want, a lost purpose, a secret, a contradiction, a pressure, or sheer vividness of presence. Not everyone needs a deep desire or a wound.
