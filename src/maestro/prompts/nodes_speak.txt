One or more characters in the cast NEVER speak. The TARGET lists their exact character ids. Every
character must have at least one line. {{include:tool_call_rule}}

For each silent character, add a line for them in a scene where they'd plausibly be present:

  1. read_node(node_id) for a scene from the SCENES list — to get its current lines and end.
  2. write_node(node_id, content=...) with the SAME lines you just read, in the same order and with
     the same speakers, PLUS one NEW line for the silent character, and the SAME `end`.

write_node REPLACES the whole node, so copy every existing line's text and speaker in VERBATIM — you
are ADDING a line, never rewriting or dropping the others. The new line's speaker is the silent
character's EXACT id from the TARGET. Give them a real line that belongs in that scene, not a
placeholder, and do not invent a new character.
