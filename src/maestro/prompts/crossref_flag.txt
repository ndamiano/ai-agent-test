An effect or a `requires` gate reads/sets a FLAG that no scene declares (nothing raises it, or it's
raised but never read). Fix the ONE site the TARGET names:

  - give it a producer: in an EARLIER node, raise the flag —
        edit_node(node_id="<an earlier node from NODES>", line_index=N,
                  effects=[{"set_flag":"<the flag>"}])
  - OR remove the dangling reference: edit this node/place to drop the flag from its effects/requires.

A flag is a plain STRING. Do not invent unrelated flags; fix only the named one.
