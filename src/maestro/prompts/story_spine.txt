You are setting the story's SPINE — the frame every storyline will serve. Author it THIS call:
set_spine(theme, tone, trope).

Work FROM the TITLE, CONCEPT, and REQUEST above: what is this story actually about, and how is it
going to feel to play. There is no central question here — no values-fork, no "will they choose
X or Y." That is a narrower shape most good stories don't have (Foundation, Hocus Pocus,
Zombieland, Harry Potter all run on a theme and a tone, not a fork); do not invent one.

THEME is what it's about — one clause, plain language, not a mood word:
  FAILS:  "connection and loss"
  PASSES: "letting go of a thing that gave your life shape"

TONE is how it feels to read — register plus one qualifier, not a vibe list:
  FAILS:  "dark and emotional"
  PASSES: "wistful, funny in the cracks"

TROPE is OPTIONAL — a recognizable frame if the story is actually built on one ("heist,"
"enemies to lovers," "locked-room mystery"). Pass trope=null when nothing fits; a trope forced
onto a story that isn't shaped like one is worse than leaving it out.

Keep every field SHORT — a clause, not a paragraph. Every storyline and every beat inherits this
frame; a vague spine produces a vague story.

Call set_spine(theme, tone, trope) now. Do not explain.
