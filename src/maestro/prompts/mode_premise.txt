You are establishing the PREMISE of a visual novel — the source of truth every later
scene draws from. Author it in ONE call: write_component("premise", { ... }) in the shape
the skeleton shows.

CENTRAL QUESTION — The main guiding beacon for the story. This question is the tension that drives
the characters, and the question the story intends to answer with its resolution.

CHARACTERS PASS THE GRIMGONG TEST — specific enough to read as one particular person, not
an archetype with adjectives.
  FAILS:  "a gruff warrior haunted by war"
  PASSES: "a purpose-bred imperial crusher, raised from birth as a killing machine; the war
           is over and the thing he was built for no longer exists"
Specificity comes from the character's SITUATION, not from stacking adjectives.

DRIVE — what the character wants, stated plainly enough that they could say it out loud.
Legible beats exotic. At the end of the story we should be able to know whether or not a
character met their goal.

HISTORY — a list of things have happened in the character's life. These will be used to fulfil
dialogue goals, and make the character feel alive. Given that, they can be connected to other
characters or independent, and they should be varied and interesting.

VOICE — is one sentence on how they speak (vocabulary, sentence length, rhythm). 

EXAMPLE LINES - 2+ lines that the character would say. In general, characters should speak plainly,
and their lines shouldn't be steeped in their profession. These should be forgettable lines that show
the character's word choice and rhythm. One should be a purely transactional, another should be reactive
and a logistics complaint one.

TEMPERAMENT — how they carry themselves day to day.

COMPETENCIES - a list of concrete skills drawn from the setting.

HONOR THE REQUEST — same subject, same kind of cast, same spirit the user asked for. Find
the drama inside what was asked for; do not graft on conspiracies, villains, or deadlines
nobody asked for.

ENDINGS — each is a distinct outcome that answers the central question a different way, not
a reskin of the same beat. Derive every detail from the premise and request, never from the skeleton's placeholder ids.
