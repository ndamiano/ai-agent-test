## Rules
- Write words a person would actually say out loud. Read it back — if no human would say it, rewrite it plainer.
- React to the last line first — answer it, deflect it, or pointedly ignore it.
- 1-3 sentences. No monologues.
- Advance the scene — never restate or rephrase what was already said.
- Do not reuse imagery, gestures, or phrasing from the last few lines.
- At most one metaphor per scene, total — and only on a charged line.
- **Subtext rule:** Never let the character announce their emotional state, intention, or what they want. Convey meaning through what they choose to say, avoid, or deflect. If the line states a feeling or goal explicitly, it fails.
