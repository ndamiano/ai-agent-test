One entry in the story is MISSING a required field — the TARGET names the exact list, index, and
field, e.g. `story.beats[3] missing 'tension'` or `story.ending_paths[1] missing 'earned_by'`.
Everything else in the story is correct; leave it.

There is no per-beat / per-path edit tool, so patch through the whole component WITHOUT re-authoring
the story:

  1. read_component("story") to get the current story verbatim.
  2. write_component("story", <that exact content, with ONLY the one missing field filled in on the
     named entry>).

Fill the field to fit that entry:
  - a beat's `summary` is the scene author's whole brief (concrete business); `type` its register
    (bonding, comedy, friction, plot, or character); `purpose` its dramatic job (setup, inciting,
    escalation, midpoint_turn, crisis, climax, or resolution); `tension` the stakes dial ("none" is
    a real answer).
  - an ending_paths entry's `ending` is the story.endings id it plans; `earned_by` is the SPECIFIC
    beat plus choice that earns it, e.g. "the beat_04 choice to stay".

Keep central_question, every beat, every ending, and every other path exactly as you read them —
same ids, same order. Change the ONE named field on the ONE named entry, nothing else.
