## Ren'Py Syntax Reference
label scene_id:                      — first line of every scene (required)
    scene bg_id with dissolve        — set background (omit "with dissolve" for very first scene)
    show char_id at left             — show character on left side
    show char_id at right            — show character on right side
    show char_id at center           — show character centered (use only when alone)
    hide char_id                     — hide character sprite
    "Narrator text."                 — narration, no character prefix (never write Narrator "text" — Narrator is not defined)
    char_id "Dialogue line."         — character speaks (use the id, not the name)
    menu:                            — player choice (only when scene has choices)
        "Choice text A":
            jump scene_id_a
        "Choice text B":
            jump scene_id_b
    jump scene_id                    — go to next scene (use when no choices)
    "The End."                       — only in the final scene
    return                           — only in the final scene, after "The End."

## Common mistakes — NEVER do these

BAD:  She adjusts the brim of her cap.
GOOD: "She adjusts the brim of her cap."

BAD:  *She presses her lower lip.*
GOOD: "She presses her lower lip."

BAD:  "char_id" "Dialogue here."
GOOD: char_id "Dialogue here."

BAD:  char_id Dialogue without quotes.
GOOD: char_id "Dialogue without quotes."

BAD:  char_id "Dialogue," she said.
GOOD: char_id "Dialogue."

BAD:  The End.
GOOD: "The End."

BAD:  "scene bg_lab with dissolve"
GOOD: scene bg_lab with dissolve

Every line inside a label block MUST be: a quoted string, char_id "text", a Ren'Py keyword, or a menu option. Bare prose and asterisks are syntax errors.
