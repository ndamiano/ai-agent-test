Rules:
- speaker: a character id from the list above, or "narrator" (use narrator 1-3 times — a scene-setting opener or a charged pause, never plot summary)
- Honor adjacency: when a line is a question, accusation, or demand aimed at someone, the next line is usually that person answering — or pointedly refusing to
- A character may speak twice in a row only when pressing or doubling down
- intent: the move, not the words — "deflects with a joke", "drops the evidence on the table", "answers a different question than the one asked"
- emotion: the speaker's state in that moment — pick states that activate differently as the scene escalates
- register: most lines are "plain" — ordinary spoken words. Mark at most 2 lines per scene "charged" — the moments where emotion breaks through and the character's voice shows its colors. A scene that is all charged is melodrama.
- The final 1-2 lines must steer directly into the scene's exit
- Every character listed gets a character_objectives entry
