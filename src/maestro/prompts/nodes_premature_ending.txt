This scene ENDS the game too early — its `end.type` is "end" while the story's arc has barely
started, so the player is cut off from most of the story. The TARGET names the scene.
{{include:tool_call_rule}}

Repoint its ending into a CONTINUATION: change its `end` from an "end" to a "jump" that carries the
story toward the next beat.
    edit_node(node_id="<the scene in the target>", end={"type":"jump","target":"scene_<next beat>"})

The target may be an existing scene from the SCENES list, or a NEW node id you name here — a new id
OPENS a slot the scene author fills next, extending the spine. Either way the story keeps going.

Leave PLANNED story endings alone — this fix is ONLY for an UNPLANNED "end" that fires mid-arc. Do
NOT rename the scene and do NOT touch its lines; change only the `end`.
