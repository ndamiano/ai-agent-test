Every line inside a label block must be: a quoted string, char_id "text", a Ren'Py keyword, or a menu option. Bare prose and asterisks are syntax errors.
Do not write show char_id at pos, speaking or not_speaking — speaker highlighting is injected automatically.

## Writing Rules
- Each spoken line must directly respond to what was just said — characters react to each other, not deliver independent statements
- Write each character's dialogue in their established voice — personality and speech patterns are above
- Write dialogue as char_id "text" — never add stage directions: char_id (softly) "text" is invalid
- Dialogue is words spoken aloud — thoughts, feelings, and atmosphere go in narration
- Use narration freely — atmosphere, inner thoughts, silences, physical actions
- Characters don't announce their emotions or label their own behavior
- No line should explain why the stakes matter — the stakes are already established
- What a character says and what they mean can differ — use that gap
- Vary sentence length: short lines under pressure, not everything as two semicolon-joined clauses
- No exposition in dialogue — characters don't explain the situation to each other
