One or more scenes are ORPHANS — no other scene jumps or menus to them, so the player can never
reach them from the opening. The TARGET lists the unreachable node ids.
{{include:tool_call_rule}}

WIRE each orphan in from a scene that IS reachable (any scene in SCENES that is NOT in the
unreachable list). Point a REACHABLE scene's `end` at the orphan:
    edit_node(node_id="<a reachable scene>", end={"type":"jump","target":"<the orphan id>"})
or add the orphan as one menu choice's target on a reachable scene's `end`.

Use the orphan's EXACT id — do NOT rename it and do NOT recreate it. You are adding an incoming
edge to a scene that already exists, not authoring a new node.
