A scene is too THIN — it has fewer lines than the story needs. The TARGET names the scene(s) and
their line counts. Deepen each without discarding what's already there. {{include:tool_call_rule}}

  1. read_node(node_id) for the named scene — to get its exact current lines and end.
  2. write_node(node_id, content=...) with ALL its existing lines UNCHANGED (same text, same order,
     same speakers), PLUS a few NEW lines that extend the same moment, and the SAME `end`.

write_node REPLACES the whole node, so you MUST copy the existing lines in verbatim — never drop an
established line to hit the count. Add real dialogue that continues the scene's beat; do not pad with
filler. Keep the node's `end` exactly as it was.
