Brief: {notes}

Length: {length} ({scene_count} scenes)

Design the narrative spine of this story. Be specific and concrete — this will be used to generate every location, character, and scene. Vague output produces incoherent stories.

Output a JSON object with these fields:

    - label: short name (e.g. "reunion", "first crack", "confession", "aftermath")

    - location_type: what KIND of place this beat needs (e.g. "somewhere public and neutral", "a place with personal history", "somewhere isolated, no escape")
- location_needs: array of distinct location types this story requires, derived from story_beats. Keep it tight — {scene_count} scenes should use 2-4 distinct locations max. Each entry:
